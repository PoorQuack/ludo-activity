      How to play

      🎲 Rules

      • Tap the dice in the middle of the board to roll. Roll a 6 to bring a token out of base.
      • Roll a 6 and you keep it and roll again. Then you play every roll: tap a small dice to choose which one to use.
      • Capturing a token or getting a token home gives you another turn, on top of any 6s.
      • Land on an opponent to send their token back to base. If two tokens share a square, only one goes back. The ★ squares and coloured start squares are safe.
      • You need the exact roll to reach home. Get all 4 tokens home to win.
      • You have 30 seconds per turn, then the game moves for you. Run out of time 3 turns in a row and you're removed from the game.

      👥 Modes

      • 2 Players: head-to-head from opposite corners.
      • 4 Players: the classic board.
      • 6 Players: a hexagon board with a longer track.

      The host picks the mode in the lobby. Bots can fill empty seats.

      🪙 Coins

      • Finish a game to earn coins: 100 for 1st, 60 for 2nd, 40 for 3rd, then 30 / 25 / 20.
      • +10 for every token you capture.
      • Games where you're the only human pay half.
      • Spend coins in the shop on tokens, dice, boards and emotes. Your board theme is just for you: everyone plays on the board they picked.
