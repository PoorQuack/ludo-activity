                How to play

                🚪 Rooms

                • Play now on the menu puts you straight into an open game, or starts a new public one if there isn't one.
                • Or create a room: public rooms show up for everyone, private ones only for people in your Discord call.
                • Anyone else can join with the room's 4-letter code (shown under the board).
                • Friends: everyone you play a game with shows under Played with. Add them as friends, then invite them to your room: it pops up in their game if they're on, or arrives as a Discord DM.
                • Under ☰ in a room: mute anyone (just for you). The host can also turn typed chat off, and kick people before the game starts. Once a game is under way, only players who have disconnected can be kicked.

                🎲 Rules

                • Tap the dice in the middle of the board to roll. Roll a 6 to bring a token out of base. Stuck with every token in base for 5 dead turns? Your next roll is a guaranteed 6.
                • Roll a 6 and you keep it and roll again, but three 6s in a row skips your turn. Then you play every roll: tap a pin and pick which dice to move it with.
                • On the 6-player board (and 3 vs 3) you roll two dice at once and play each one. A throw with a 6 rolls again, and a 6 in three throws in a row skips your turn.
                • Land on an opponent's token to send it back to base. If several colours share the square, every lone token there goes back.
                • Safe spots: the ★ squares, the coloured start squares, and any square where you have two or more tokens together (a pair). Nobody can capture tokens there. Anyone can still pass over or land on them.
                • Every token you capture, and every token you get home, gives you another turn, on top of any 6s.
                • Revenge: whoever captures you gets a red target on their tokens (only you see it). Capture one of them back for +5 coins at the end of the game.
                • Capture two tokens in a row without being captured yourself for a Double, three for a Rampage. Everyone in the room sees it.
                • You need the exact roll to reach home. Get all your tokens home to win (4, or 2 in a quick game).
                • You have 30 seconds per turn, then the game moves for you. Run out of time 3 turns in a row and you're removed from the game.

                👥 Modes

                • 2 Players: head-to-head from opposite corners.
                • 4 Players: the classic board.
                • 6 Players: a hexagon board with a longer track.
                • 2 vs 2 and 3 vs 3: team games. Teammates sit opposite each other (Team A and Team B on the seat cards). They never capture each other, and a teammate's token on your square keeps you both safe. Every finishing place scores points for your team (2 vs 2: 1st 3, 2nd 2, 3rd 1, last 0; 3 vs 3: 5 down to 0), and the team with more points wins. A tie goes to a decider: one colour per team, teammates take turns rolling it, and the first colour with all four home wins. Every seat must be filled (bots count).

                The host picks the mode in the lobby. Bots can fill empty seats.

                • Quick game: the host can switch it on under the mode in the lobby. Everyone plays with 2 tokens instead of 4, so games are about twice as fast.

                🪙 Coins

                • Finish a game to earn coins: 25 for 1st, 15 for 2nd, 10 for 3rd, then 8 / 6 / 5. If everyone else surrenders before anyone has got all their tokens home, placements pay for how far your tokens got — a game handed over isn't worth full price.
                • +2 for every token you capture.
                • Games against bots only (you're the only human) don't pay coins. If every player leaves a game, it waits a minute for someone to come back, then ends with no coins. Earnings stop at 120 coins a day (the limit resets at midnight UTC).
                • Every game against other people also scores leaderboard points: 10 for each player you finish ahead of, plus 10 for beating them all (bots don't count). Only games count for the leaderboards. The top 3 each week win 300 / 200 / 100 coins, and each month 1000 / 600 / 300.
                • Invite friends: share the game from the Friends card. When someone new opens it through your invite and finishes their first game, you get 50 coins. Every new player counts.
                • Daily quests: three new ones every day at midnight UTC. Each one pays coins, and finishing all three pays a bonus. Quest coins don't count towards the daily limit.
                • Colour-blind? Pick Protanopia, Deuteranopia or Tritanopia in ⚙ Settings (top right, on the menu and in a game): every player gets colours chosen for that kind of colour blindness, and a shape of their own.
                • Badges: tap your name at the top of the menu to see them. Every level pays coins once, and getting one to gold unlocks a title to wear under your name.
                • Daily chest: once a day, roll the d20 on the menu for 5 coins a pip (5 to 100). It resets at midnight UTC.

                🛍️ Shop

                • Spend coins on tokens, dice, boards, taunts and emotes. Your board theme is just for you: everyone plays on the board they picked.
                • Tap Try it on anything to see it in action before you buy it: rolls, moves, captures and wins.
                • Today's deal: one item a day is 30% off, the same for everyone. It's on the menu and at the top of its tab in the shop.
                • Taunts play on the board whenever you capture someone, and everyone in the room sees them.
                • Gifts: open an item while you're in a room with other people and pick who to give it to. You pay, they get it.
