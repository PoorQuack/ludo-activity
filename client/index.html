        How to play

        🚪 Rooms

        • Create room on the menu: public rooms show up for everyone, private ones only for people in your Discord call.
        • Anyone else can join with the room's 4-letter code (shown under the board).
        • Under ☰ in a room: mute anyone (just for you). The host can also turn typed chat off, and kick people before the game starts. Once a game is under way, only players who have disconnected can be kicked.

        🎲 Rules

        • Tap the dice in the middle of the board to roll. Roll a 6 to bring a token out of base. Stuck with every token in base for 5 dead turns? Your next roll is a guaranteed 6.
        • Roll a 6 and you keep it and roll again, but three 6s in a row skips your turn. Then you play every roll: tap a pin and pick which dice to move it with.
        • Land on an opponent's token to send it back to base. If several colours share the square, every lone token there goes back.
        • Safe spots: the ★ squares, the coloured start squares, and any square where you have two or more tokens together (a pair). Nobody can capture tokens there. Anyone can still pass over or land on them.
        • Every token you capture, and every token you get home, gives you another turn, on top of any 6s.
        • You need the exact roll to reach home. Get all 4 tokens home to win.
        • You have 30 seconds per turn, then the game moves for you. Run out of time 3 turns in a row and you're removed from the game.

        👥 Modes

        • 2 Players: head-to-head from opposite corners.
        • 4 Players: the classic board.
        • 6 Players: a hexagon board with a longer track.

        The host picks the mode in the lobby. Bots can fill empty seats.

        🪙 Coins

        • Finish a game to earn coins: 25 for 1st, 15 for 2nd, 10 for 3rd, then 8 / 6 / 5. If the game ends early because someone surrenders, placements pay for how far your tokens got — a game handed over isn't worth full price.
        • +2 for every token you capture.
        • Games against bots only (you're the only human) don't pay coins. If every player leaves a game, it waits a minute for someone to come back, then ends with no coins. Earnings stop at 120 coins a day (the limit resets at midnight UTC).
        • Every game against other people also scores leaderboard points: 10 for each player you finish ahead of, plus 10 for beating them all (bots don't count). Your three daily quests add points too (new ones every day at midnight UTC; finish all three for a bonus). The top 3 each week win 300 / 200 / 100 coins, and each month 1000 / 600 / 300.
        • Spend coins in the shop on tokens, dice, boards and emotes. Your board theme is just for you: everyone plays on the board they picked.
        • Colour-blind? Turn on Colour-blind mode on the menu or under ☰ in a game: every player gets safer colours and a shape of their own.
