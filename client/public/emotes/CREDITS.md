# Sticker credits

Sticker art in this folder comes from emoji packs shared on [emoji.gg](https://emoji.gg/packs).
The artists keep the rights to their work. If you made one of these and want it credited
differently or taken down, open an issue and it will be removed.

## Our own stickers

`brainrot/` (Brainrot pack) and `dicerot/` (Dice brainrot pack) are original art made for this
game: drawn as SVG in `tools/stickers/brainrot.mjs` and rendered with `render.mjs` + `pack.py`.
They belong to the game, so they can be sold.
