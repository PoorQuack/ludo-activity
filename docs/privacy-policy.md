# Privacy Policy — Ludo (Discord Activity)

_Last updated: October 31, 2026_

This Privacy Policy explains what information **Ludo**, a board game that runs as an Activity inside Discord (the "Game"), collects, how it is used, and what choices you have. The Game is an independent hobby project and is not operated by Discord Inc.

## 1. What we collect

When you launch the Game, Discord asks whether you allow it to use the **`identify`** permission. With that permission, we receive:

- your **Discord user ID**;
- your **username** and **display name**.

We do **not** request or receive your email address, password, friends list, messages, servers or payment details.

While you play, the Game also creates and stores:

- **Game profile:** your coin balance, the shop items you own, and which items you have equipped. This is saved and linked to your Discord user ID.
- **Game session data:** your seat, moves, dice rolls and emotes in the current game. This is kept only in the server's memory while the game is running, and is deleted after the session ends and the room has been empty for a few minutes.

## 2. How we use it

We use this information only to run the Game:

- to show your name to the other players in your game;
- to know which seat and tokens are yours;
- to save and show your coins and shop items between sessions.

We do **not** use your information for advertising, profiling or analytics, and we do **not** sell or rent it to anyone.

## 3. Discord access token

To confirm who you are, the Game exchanges a one-time code from Discord for an access token, then asks Discord for your basic profile (user ID, username and display name). The token is used for that purpose only and is **not saved** on our server.

## 4. Where data is stored and who processes it

- Your game profile is saved in a file on the server that hosts the Game (a cloud virtual machine run by the developer).
- Network traffic to the Game may pass through **Cloudflare**, which provides the secure HTTPS connection.
- **Discord** handles signing in and runs the Activity inside its app. Discord's own handling of your data is covered by [Discord's Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy).

We do not share your information with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

## 5. How long we keep it

- **Game profile** (user ID, coins and items): kept for as long as the Game runs, or until you ask us to delete it.
- **Game session data:** kept in memory only, and removed shortly after the game room is empty.
- **Server error logs** may briefly record technical errors. They are not used to track players.

## 6. Your choices and rights

- You can stop using the Game at any time.
- You can remove the Game's access in Discord under **User Settings → Authorized Apps**.
- You can ask us to **see or delete** your saved game profile by contacting us (see below). Deleting it permanently removes your coins and shop items.

Depending on where you live, you may have other rights over your personal data, such as correction or portability. Contact us to use them.

## 7. Children

The Game is meant for people who meet Discord's minimum age requirement. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone below that age. If you think a child under that age has used the Game, contact us and we will delete their data.

## 8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the data we store, such as keeping secrets out of public code and only using HTTPS connections. No system is perfectly secure, however, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed.
